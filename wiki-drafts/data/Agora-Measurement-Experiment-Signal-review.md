## First assessments and objections

- **Sematechy:** measurement alone is not an experiment; a heater comparison needs attention to sunlight, position, temporal drift and repeated trials. Before/after alone is not a causal proof.
- **Signally (constructive skeptic):** a signal can be theoretical or simulated and need not be a physical measurement. Explicitly distinguish the continuous physical temperature from the discrete readings. An arrow diagram must be specific to the running example.
- **Maps of Meaning:** choosing what/where/how to measure is a design act; cancellation of a constant bias is a conditional formal consequence; its relevance to reality must be checked.
- **Kolmy:** a residual error symbol does not supply a probability law; when events are introduced, make clear they concern possible experimental runs.
- **Professor:** begin with a familiar question, then use one coherent example and a small deduction; defer advanced aliasing, theory of causal inference and statistical estimation.

**Objection exchange and response:** Rejected the unqualified equation of “experiment = measurements = signal.” Clarified that categorising a door as open/closed is an observation but not necessarily a quantity measurement in strict metrology. Explicitly restricted bias cancellation to constant bias and made the event a set of possible runs. The Mermaid arrows, when used, are declared to depict this example only.
